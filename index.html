Our Story

Crafting Culinary Excellence Since 2008

Founded by Chef Marcus Aurelius, Aurum & Ember began as a humble dream to redefine fine dining. What started as a small bistro has evolved into one of the most celebrated restaurants in the city.

Our philosophy is simple: source the finest seasonal ingredients, honor traditional techniques, and present each dish as a work of art. Every plate that leaves our kitchen carries the passion and precision of our world-class culinary team.

With over 15 years of experience, Chef Aurelius has trained under Michelin-starred masters across Europe and Asia, bringing a unique fusion of flavors that has earned us numerous accolades.
